About

Flawina Makeup Academy

At Flawina Makeup Academy, we believe that makeup is more than just beauty it's confidence, creativity, and self expression. Inspired by the world of K Beauty, our academy is dedicated to teaching modern, skin first makeup techniques that enhance natural beauty while building professional skills. Whether you're a beginner, a beauty enthusiast, or an aspiring makeup artist, our expert led courses are designed to help you master the art of flawless makeup with confidence.

Our training combines hands on practical learning with industry expertise, covering skin preparation, product selection, complexion perfection, eye makeup, bridal artistry, and the latest beauty trends. With personalized guidance, small batch sizes, and real world techniques, we ensure every student receives the support needed to grow and excel.

At Flawina Makeup Academy, we don't just teach makeup we inspire confidence, nurture creativity, and prepare you for a successful career in the beauty industry. Join a community dedicated to excellence, continuous learning, and transforming passion into profession.

ABOUT FOUNDER

Naina Thapa

Naina Thapa is the Founder of Flawina Makeup Academy and a renowned Skin Analyst and Professional Makeup Expert with over 9 years of experience in the beauty and skincare industry. Trained under leading Korean beauty artist Jung Saem Mool. She specializes in K Beauty inspired makeup, Korean drama inspired base makeup, eye makeup, skin analysis, colour analysis, advanced concealer techniques, and professional skin preparation. Her expertise combines international beauty trends with practical industry knowledge, creating flawless, skin first looks that enhance natural beauty while delivering long-lasting results.

With a passion for educating and inspiring future beauty professionals, Naina Thapa established Flawina Makeup Academy to provide world class makeup education rooted in creativity, precision, and professionalism. Her hands on teaching approach empowers students with the confidence, technical expertise, and real world skills needed to succeed in today's beauty industry. Through personalized mentorship and a commitment to excellence, she continues to shape the next generation of makeup artists, helping them transform their passion into a successful and rewarding career.
